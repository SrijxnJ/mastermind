# Lesson 01: The Triple Hook Method

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/p/De_5mQ0R8PE/ (2 min 14 s). The video refers to Kallaway's full hook masterclass on YouTube (700K+ views).
- **Studied:** 27 Oct 2026, with a scene-by-scene AI walkthrough (vidIQ).
- **Genre:** education / talking head with motion graphics

## The lesson: three steps for a hook nobody skips

### Step 1: Context (the first words)
- Viewers only stay if they have a stake in the topic. Say **what the video is about immediately**.
- Deliver the context **three ways at once**: audio (what you say), visuals (what's on screen) and on-screen text/captions.
- The creator's data claim: delaying the context even to the **back half of the first sentence** sharply increases skip rate.

### Step 2: The Lean (create curiosity)
Make the viewer want the next sentence. Three ways:
1. **Proof:** a stat, number, screenshot or result.
2. **Pain point / benefit:** name a specific struggle they have, or an outcome they want.
3. **Something shocking:** an unexpected image, statement or fact.

**Check:** is the context instantly clear, and is there a reason to lean in? He calls the first two steps together the **"Context Lean"**.

### Step 3: The Contrarian Snapback
Let the viewer settle into one expectation, then **yank them the other way**. His analogy: setting a fishing hook means letting the fish swim one way, then pulling the line in the opposite direction.

Context, then lean, then snapback is what he calls the **"God-tier hook formula"**.

## How the video itself is made (techniques to steal)
| Area | What he does |
|---|---|
| Hook (0–3 s) | A bold claim ("scientifically impossible to skip") plus instant visual proof (a retention-graph graphic) |
| Pacing | A visual change (cut, layout swap or animation) every **1.5–2.5 s** |
| Layout | Split screen: graphic/example on top, talking head at the bottom; switches to a full-screen face for key lines |
| Text | Clean sans-serif (Inter / SF Pro style), white and yellow on dark, **red = bad, green = good** highlights |
| Effects | Frosted-glass UI cards, drawn diagrams (curves, radar, fishing line), red circles and arrows to guide the eye |
| Sound | Low ambient synth bed throughout, plus SFX on every visual event: whoosh (move), pop (text in), chime (green check), thud/click (card change) |
| Grade/light | Dark matte background, warm key light on face, gold rim light |
| CTA | Comment-to-DM: "Comment 'hooks' and I'll send it to you." Social proof (700K views) raises the value of the freebie |

**Gym/travel version of his sound design:** one music bed for the whole video, plus a small SFX on every text pop or counter change (pop, whoosh, thud on the final rep). It works in Edits through its SFX library.

## Takeaways (added to the Brain)
- [Content & hooks] Context in the first words, through all 3 channels (say + show + write).
- [Content & hooks] Lean with proof, pain/benefit or shock. Snap back with the opposite of what they expect.
- [Editing & pacing] Change something on screen every 1.5–2.5 s.
- [Sound] Bed + event SFX: whoosh = movement, pop = text, chime = success, thud = impact.
- [Text & fonts] Clean sans for teaching content; red/green colour coding for wrong/right.
- [Growth] Comment-keyword CTA ("comment X and I'll send it"), backed by social proof.
