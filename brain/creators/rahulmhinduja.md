# Creator study: @rahulmhinduja (Rahul Hinduja)

- **Studied:** 27 Oct 2026
- **Sample:** the **12 most recent reels** that the vidIQ listing returns (it caps at 12). The brief asked for 30; this file covers **12 of 30**. See *Gaps and caveats*.
- **Method:** all 12 reels were downloaded locally with yt-dlp for analysis only. They are not stored in this repo and must not be redistributed. Each reel was then analysed with:
  - ffmpeg scene-cut detection
  - an OpenCV frame-difference "visual beat" count
  - a 1 fps contact sheet for the whole reel, plus a 4 fps sheet of the first 3 s, read by eye
  - EBU R128 loudness and an audio noise-floor check
  - a Whisper (small) language check and rough English translation of the first 15 s
  - vidIQ credits used: **0**
- **Labels:** **[measured]** = computed from the files or the metrics. **[seen]** = read off the contact sheets. **[inferred]** = *(our take)*.

That means the mix is heavily compressed and consistent, and matches common streaming loudness targets *(the identical values suggest normalisation, by the editor or Instagram: inferred)*.
- **Pauses:** fewer than 2.5% of 100 ms windows are more than 25 dB below the median level **[measured]**. Breaths and pauses are cut out; there is no dead air.
- **Background under the voice:** the quietest 5% of the audio sits 9–21 dB under the speech median, never at true silence **[measured]**. ## 3. What drives his outliers

### 11.3M: Flipkart "4 Impossible Deals" (22× followers)

- **Measured:** like rate **0.3%** and only 334 comments on 11.3M plays. That is roughly 10–17× lower engagement per play than his organic reels.
- **Inferred:** this pattern fits **paid amplification**, either a partnership ad boosted by the brand or a placement on Flipkart's side. It is not organic virality.
- **Supporting factors:** a festive moment (Big Billion Days / Diwali), mass-market products (smartwatches, Ray-Ban Meta), the highest production value and fastest pace in the sample (1.2 s beats).
- **Takeaway:** **don't use this reel as a benchmark** for organic reach.

### 1.2M: Gmail one-word trick (2.38× followers, best organic)

- **The audience is everyone**, not PC builders. Everybody has a spammy Gmail inbox. This is the only reel in the sample whose topic has no hardware barrier *(our take)*.
- **Tiny effort, instant payoff:** "change just one word" (#inbox → #sub). Viewers can try it on the spot, which encourages saves and shares *(inferred; saves and shares not visible)*.
- **Clean Layout-A motion graphics:** the URL bar is the hero object and the idea is shown, not only told.
- **A strong lead magnet** ("Top 10 Gmail shortcuts") gave 2.7K comments.
- **Like rate is low (1.8%)** **[measured]**. That is typical when reach comes from non-followers who take the tip and leave *(inferred)*.

### 552K: ₹1.5L Gaming & Streaming build (1.09×, best like rate at scale)

- **Budget sweet spot:** mid-budget (₹1.5L) is aspirational but realistic for the core audience *(our take)*.
- **Contrarian hook:** "8 GB GPU in 2026 is a waste of money" contradicts what shops push.
- **Proof on screen:** TechSpot article highlights ("8 GB of VRAM is simply not enough"), a VRAM bar comparison, and shop price tags with dates.
- **Meme rhythm:** Joker "burning money", Messi trophy kiss with the GPU, and a DiCaprio toast that becomes the cover.
- **Timeliness:** sits inside the "prices rising weekly" narrative.
- **Result:** 26.6K likes (4.8%) and 2.3K comments **[measured]**.

### Weaker reels and likely reasons *(our take, unless marked)*

- **ChatGPT prompts (0.14×):**
  - a debunk ("there are no cheat codes") leaves the viewer with less, not more
  - AI-prompt content is saturated
  - the topic sits off-pillar for a PC/hardware audience
- **Meld sponsored (0.17×):** 161 s long, an unfamiliar product, and the subject is streaming setups, which is niche even within his audience.
- **Photocraft (0.22×, 42 s):**
  - shortest reel
  - a niche "free Photoshop" tool for creative users
  - high comment ratio (23%) **[measured]**, so the people who saw it wanted it, but reach stayed low
- **₹75K build (0.22×):** it was **posted on the day of this study**, so the numbers are not comparable yet.
- **Google Authenticator (0.51×):** good reach for an explainer, but no keyword CTA. Comments collapsed to 88.

**Pattern:**
- **PC-build reels** have the most loyal engagement (highest like rates).
- **Universal everyday-tech tricks** have the biggest non-follower reach.
- **AI/how-to "debunk" content** is weakest.

## 5. Gaps and caveats

- **12 of 30.**
  - The listing returned 12 reels. Two of them are pinned and older (6 and 17 Oct), so the organic sample is really **10 reels from 18–27 Oct**.
  - Patterns from one month (and one news cycle: the RAM/GPU price spike) may not hold for his wider catalogue.
- **Metrics are a snapshot (27 Oct 2026).**
  - Reel 3 was hours old.
  - Some numbers differ slightly between vidIQ and yt-dlp: yt-dlp shows 181 comments on the Flipkart reel vs vidIQ 334, and 5,776 likes on reel 3. The table uses vidIQ.
- **Not visible:** saves, shares, reach split (followers vs non-followers), watch time, retention curves, and any ad spend. Every claim about boosting or shareability is **inferred**.
- **Transcripts:**
  - Only the first 15 s were machine-translated (Whisper small, Hindi to English). The wording is approximate and some words are garbled ("Hissipiti library", "big billiards").
  - The exact Hinglish lines quoted above come from **on-screen captions** where they existed (10, 11, 12).
  - Full transcripts were not produced.
- **Pace metrics are approximate.**
  - Hard-cut detection under-counts smooth motion-graphic animation (Layout A).
  - The "visual beat" metric depends on its threshold. Contact sheets were used to sanity-check both.
- **Audio:**
  - The presence of a music bed is inferred from the noise floor, not confirmed by listening.
  - No music track is declared in the metadata.
- **Covers:** the descriptions come from the vidIQ listing. Cover images were not downloaded separately; the match to in-reel payoff frames is from the contact sheets.
- **Font names are not identifiable** from video frames; only styles are described.
- **Comment-CTA effect:** it rests on one no-CTA reel (Google Authenticator). It is a strong signal but n = 1.
- **Downloaded videos:** kept only in the session scratchpad for analysis, not committed, not to be redistributed.
