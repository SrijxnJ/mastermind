# Meme sounds and formats (2026 field guide)

70 meme sounds and formats in use in gym, tech/PC, gaming and travel reels. For each one: what it sounds like, what it means, the exact frame rule at **30 fps**, the visual pairing, the niche fit, **where to get it legally**, and the risks.

**How to read this:**
- **Edits / CapCut search** is the licensed route. Sounds in the in-app libraries are cleared for use *inside that app's export*. Search the exact term given. Availability changes by region and account type: Business accounts get a reduced music library, so switch to a Creator account for music memes.
- **CC0 in library** marks a file in `library/sfx/` you can use anywhere. ✅ **recreation** means a CC0 version of the same thing (a censor beep, a sad-trombone motif, a CC0 announcer line). ≈ **substitute** means a similar-feel CC0 sound, not the meme itself. The audience will not read it as the meme.
- Never download meme audio from YouTube/TikTok rippers, myinstants or soundboard sites. Those files are copyrighted recordings (films, games, TV, creators' voices).
- Frame numbers are at 30 fps (1 frame = 33 ms). "Lands on" means the sound's transient (or peak) sits on that frame.
- Trend status as of Oct 2026: classics (vine boom, bruh, metal pipe) are evergreen but overused. 'Fahhh' peaked on TikTok from Sept 2025 (Know Your Meme). Aura farming and phonk edits are still common but saturated. Check Instagram's trending audio (the ↗ arrow) or TikTok Creative Center before relying on any "new" sound.
- Sources: Know Your Meme (fahh, aura, yara-yara phonk, sleep phonk entries); voxbooster/uppbeat/dubbingai 2026 meme-sound roundups (vendor lists, not usage data). See the bottom of this file.

<a id="vine-boom"></a>
### Vine boom

- **Sounds like:** One deep, reverbed bass 'BOOM' (sub thump + tail), about 1.5 s.
- **Means:** Realisation, shock, 'did he really just say that'. The universal punchline marker.
- **Timing (30 fps):** Transient lands ON the reaction/punchline frame (frame 0 of the face or text). Freeze or hold 8-12 frames (0.27-0.4 s) at 30 fps, then cut. Never let it run under speech.
- **Visual pairing:** Zoom punch to 115-120% in 2-3 frames (ease-out), optional 1-frame flash, freeze on the face, slight shake (2-4 px) for 4 frames.
- **Niches:** gym, tech, gaming, travel, general
- **Edits search:** `vine boom`  ·  **CapCut search:** `vine boom`
- **CC0 in library:** ≈ substitute: `bass-drop-boom` (similar-feel substitute, NOT the vine boom sample): `sfx/bass-drop-boom/kenney-sci-fi-lowFrequency_explosion_000.mp3`, `sfx/bass-drop-boom/oga-muffled-distant-explosion-NenadSimic_-_Muffled_Distant_Explosion.mp3`, `sfx/bass-drop-boom/kenney-sci-fi-lowFrequency_explosion_001.mp3`
- **Risk:** Overused: one per video, max. Original sample is not CC0; the library boom is a similar-feel substitute, not the meme itself.

- **Sounds like:** Flat male voice saying 'bruh' (~0.6 s).

- **Visual pairing:** Freeze + slow 105% push-in; desaturate slightly.
- **Niches:** gaming, gym, general
- **Edits search:** `bruh`  ·  **CapCut search:** `bruh`

- **Risk:** Voice clip: no CC0 recreation in library (record your own 'bruh' - it is a word, not a copyrighted performance). Dated/cringe in 2026 if used straight; works best ironic.
